The group, as I understand it

Each of the four businesses does one clear job. LunyBee seems to be what makes the expertise behind them available to people outside the family.

  1. The estate grows the coffee.
  2. HPRI turns it into lots that score high and taste unusual.
  3. Roasters put the name on the bag and carry the story to drinkers and cafés.
  4. Some of those people want to come closer: as a Golden Wood guest, a café client, a student or a producer asking how it's done.

Where a founder's office fits

Five names means five kinds of people asking for your time, often in the same week. My guess at what that looks like, and where I could help:

Who's asking
What they want
What I'd take off your plate
Roasters
Samples, lot details, harvest timing, the story for their bag
A partner tracker: who bought what, when to follow up, what's coming next season
Producers
"How did you do that ferment?" Processing advice and training
Turning enquiries into scoped proposals, and keeping a clear list of what HPRI can teach
Cafés
Consulting on menus, sourcing, setup and quality
Intake, scoping, timelines and follow-ups, so you only spend time on the actual coffee
Travellers
A stay, a tour, a cupping, a programme
Packaging Golden Wood stays and LunyBee programmes together, and keeping bookings and partners in sync
Buyers
Coffee and gear, in stores and online
Keeping track of retail partners and launches across Classic Coffees and LunyBee

None of this replaces your expertise. The point is that nothing slips while you're in the lab, at a competition table or with guests on the estate.

04

My first 90 days

A starting plan. I'd expect to change it after the first week.

Days 1–30

Learn the whole group

  • Time on the estate, in the HPRI lab and at Golden Wood
  • Map every incoming request across the five names: who it's from, how it arrives, where it waits
  • Write down the real year, harvest to harvest
Days 31–60

Build the basics

  • One tracker for roasters, cafés, producers and programmes
  • A short briefing note before each of your important meetings
  • Proposals, follow-ups and scheduling handled end to end
Days 61–90

Own one project

  • e.g. a "visit the estate on your bag" programme with roaster partners, linking their customers to Golden Wood and LunyBee
  • Or whichever project you most want moved
  • Report back with numbers, not just updates
